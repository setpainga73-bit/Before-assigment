A little about us

Good coffee should feel like an invitation.

Bean Boutique started as a neighbourhood roastery and grew into a place for people who care about the details without taking themselves too seriously.

We choose expressive beans, roast in small batches, and share what we learn so every cup at home can feel a little more intentional.
